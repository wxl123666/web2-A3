💡 Our mission

We exist to make giving easy. Too many small Hebei charities run brilliant events that nobody hears about, while thousands of residents would happily take part if they only knew. This platform closes that gap: one searchable calendar of every registered charity event in the province, with transparent fundraising progress and online registration so donors can book a place in seconds.

✓ Registered charities only ✓ Transparent fundraising goals ✓ Register in under a minute ✓ Policy-checked events
